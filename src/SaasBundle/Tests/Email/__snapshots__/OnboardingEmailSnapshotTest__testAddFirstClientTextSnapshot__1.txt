No client, no invoice. Let's start there.
============================================================

Everything starts from your client list. Add one and you can send them a quote or an invoice straight away, and follow what they owe you.

What you need
------------------------------------------------------------
Three things are enough. The rest can wait.

* The client's or their company's name
* An email address to send them their invoices
* Their SIREN if they are a company: the address is filled in from the register

Add my first client: http://localhost/clients/add

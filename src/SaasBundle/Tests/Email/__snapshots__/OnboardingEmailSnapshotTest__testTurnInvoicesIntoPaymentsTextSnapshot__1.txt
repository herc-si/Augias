Let your clients pay in one click.
============================================================

Connect your own payment account once: your invoices are paid online, and you are told as soon as the money arrives.

An invoice paid online is settled faster than one paid by bank transfer
The easier it is to pay, the sooner you are paid.

How it works
------------------------------------------------------------

1. In Payments → Payment methods, connect your payment account (Stripe, PayPal…).
2. Every invoice you send then carries a "Pay" button.
3. Your client pays, you are told, and the invoice turns Paid.

Add a payment method: http://localhost/payments/methods

P.S. Augias takes nothing on what you collect: only your payment provider's fees apply.

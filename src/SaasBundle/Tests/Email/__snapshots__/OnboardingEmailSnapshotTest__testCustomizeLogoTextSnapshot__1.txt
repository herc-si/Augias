Your logo, your colours, your invoices.
============================================================

Your clients see your invoice before they read it. With your logo and colour, it carries your name, not a software's.

Two settings
------------------------------------------------------------

1. Add your logo
   PNG, SVG or JPG. It shows on every quote, invoice and credit note.

2. Choose the sender name
   The name your clients see when your invoices reach their inbox.

Customise my documents: http://localhost/settings

P.S. the logo and colour also apply to your quotes and credit notes.

A client pays you every month? Augias takes care of it.
============================================================

A recurring invoice is prepared once. Augias issues it on each due date and chases late payments.

How it works
------------------------------------------------------------

1. Create the invoice once, then set its frequency and start date.

2. Augias issues and sends it on each due date.

3. Turn on reminders: late payments are followed without you thinking of it.

# Asking for help

On the hosted service, you can ask the team running it for help. Your request allows them to look at your company, read-only, for the time you choose.

:::info
This exists only if the operator of the service offers it, and is open to the company's owner and administrators. Without it, there is no `Support` entry in the menu.
:::

## Sending a request

1. In the sidebar, open `System`, then `Support`.
2. In `Your problem`, describe what you are trying to do and what happens. Do not include any password.
3. Choose `How long access stays open`. Access closes by itself at the end of this time, counted from when you send the request.
4. Click `Send the request`.

You are told by email as soon as the request is taken. While it is open, a banner reminds every member of the company, with the time access ends.

## What you allow

- The person helping you works under their own name, never as one of your users.
- Access is read-only: nothing can be changed, sent or exported.
- Your settings are visible, including the services you connected, but never your credentials with them (mail sending, payment methods, e-invoicing, notifications).
- Every page they open is recorded in your company's access log.

## Ending access

Access closes by itself when the time is up. To close it sooner, open `Support` (or click `Manage` in the banner), then `End access`. Any session under way is cut off.

## Following your requests

When the request is closed, you receive by email an account of what was done. The `Earlier requests` table on the `Support` page keeps every request, with its status (`Waiting`, `Taken`, `Closed`, `Revoked`, `Expired`), who took it, and what was done.

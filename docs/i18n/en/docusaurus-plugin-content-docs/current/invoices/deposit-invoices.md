# Billing a deposit

An accepted quote can be billed in stages: one or several deposits first, then a final invoice for what is left.

## Bill the deposit

Open the accepted quote. In the `Deposits` box, enter the percentage of the quote to bill, then click `Bill`.

The invoice form opens, already filled in: one line "Deposit of 30 % on quote no. …" per tax rate of the quote, so that each rate is billed its share. Check the invoice, choose who receives it, then save or send it like any other invoice.

A deposit invoice is a real invoice: it takes a number when it is finalised, carries its tax, and is paid and chased like the others. Its page recalls the quote it comes from.

:::info
Nothing is created until something is saved: closing the form adds no invoice and uses no number.
:::

You can bill several deposits on the same quote, as long as together they do not exceed the quote. The `Deposits` box lists them with their amount and status.

## Bill the balance

When the work is done, click `Create the invoice` on the quote. The final invoice carries every line of the quote, then one line "Deposit already billed — invoice no. …" per deposit, as a negative. Its totals are therefore what is left to bill: before tax, tax and amount due.

Only **finalised** deposits are deducted. A deposit invoice left as a draft, or cancelled, is not taken into account: finalise it or delete it before creating the final invoice.

:::warning
Once the final invoice exists, no further deposit can be billed on that quote.
:::

## Good to know

- A percentage discount on the quote applies the same way to the deposit and to the final invoice. A fixed-amount discount is split between them in the proportion of the deposit.
- In electronic invoicing, a deposit invoice is sent as such (the "prepayment invoice" type).
